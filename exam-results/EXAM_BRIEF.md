# EXAM BRIEF - AI Architect Floor Plan Agent

**Plot Data**
- Dimensions: 30 ft (width E-W) × 60 ft (depth N-S) = 1800 sq ft
- Orientation: North-facing (front = North)
- Location type: Ajpan Nagar / typical Delhi residential colony, New Delhi
- Front road: 20 ft | Rear service road: 20 ft
- Front setback: 10 ft (North) → Buildable depth ≈ 50 ft
- Sides: Assume party wall or minimal side setback; primary light from North & South

**Building Configuration**
- Stilt parking (excluded from FAR)
- Typical floors: Upper Ground, First, Second, Third (identical 3BHK plans)
- Lintel level: 9 ft for doors & windows
- Large sliding windows preferred for natural light

**Functional Requirements**
1. Efficient Staircase + Elevator core with common landing (minimal footprint, ergonomic human movement)
2. Grand entrance into Living / Living-Dining (avoid small claustrophobic lobby)
3. 3 Bedrooms, each with attached Bathroom (minimum 5'-0" width × 8'-6" length)
4. Kitchen with natural light (preferably SE for Vastu)
5. Maximize natural light for Kitchen + all 3 Bedrooms from North and/or South faces
6. Wardrobes / walk-in closet where possible (attached to bath preferred)
7. Shafts acceptable for bathroom mechanical ventilation (hotel style)

**Vastu Guidelines (practical application)**
- Master Bedroom preferred in South-West
- No bedrooms in North-East
- Kitchen preferred South-East (Agni)
- Living preferred North / North-East
- Bathrooms avoid North-East
- Stair preferably South or West zone

**Success Criteria for Agent**
- Produce exactly 3 distinct conceptual options
- All dimensions humanly possible and proportional
- No blocked doors, no kitchen slab in front of entry, no 3 ft bathrooms
- Clear circulation, good volume feel, grandeur at entry
- Self-rate honestly; Jury (Human Architect) will score 0-10. Minimum 7/10 required to pass.

Agent must study global architecture practice, YouTube architect workflows, space planning theory, ergonomics, and Delhi bylaws before drafting.
