# OPTION 02 — Compact Central-ish Core with Dual Light Emphasis

**Concept**: Slightly more compact core shifted towards centre-West to free both North and South faces more evenly. Creates two private bedroom clusters and a stronger North-South axis for light and ventilation. Slightly tighter but higher efficiency.

**Building Footprint**: 30'-0" (E-W) × 50'-0" (N-S)

## Zoning

### Core
- Location: West-central, ~18' from North, footprint 7'-6" × 13'-0"
- Lift 5'×5' + Stair 7'-6" wide (still comfortable)
- Common landing allows access without crossing public Living

### Living + Dining
- Dimensions: 20'-0" × 16'-0" (North)
- Slightly more intimate but still open and grand relative to plot width
- Full North glazing
- Dining can spill towards East

### Kitchen
- Dimensions: 10'-0" × 11'-0" (SE)
- Strong South light + possible internal shaft for exhaust
- Direct access from Dining, secondary service door if space

### Master Bedroom (true SW)
- 12'-6" × 13'-6"
- South + West light possible
- Attached Bath 5'-6" × 9'-0"
- Walk-in 4'-6" × 6'-0"

### Bedroom 2 (NW)
- 11'-0" × 11'-6"
- North light primary
- Bath 5'-0" × 8'-6"

### Bedroom 3 (SE / South mid)
- 11'-0" × 12'-0"
- South light
- Bath 5'-0" × 8'-6" (shaft OK)

### Key Differences from Option 01
- Core more central → shorter corridors
- Living slightly smaller but still volumetric
- Better cross-ventilation potential North-South
- Slightly higher carpet efficiency

**Approximate Carpet**: ~1,150 – 1,190 sq ft

**Vastu**: Strong (SW Master, SE Kitchen, N Living)
**Light**: Excellent dual orientation
**Circulation**: Very efficient, short private corridors

**Self-Rating**: 8.0 / 10

**Notes**: Good for clients who want tighter, more efficient planning without losing the grand entry feel. Core still does not obstruct North entry.
