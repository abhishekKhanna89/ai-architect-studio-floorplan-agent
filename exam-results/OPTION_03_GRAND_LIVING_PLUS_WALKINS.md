# OPTION 03 — Maximum Grand Living + Walk-in Closets Priority

**Concept**: Sacrifice a little bedroom size to create the most impressive entry and Living volume possible on a 30' wide plot, while giving Master and one more bedroom proper walk-in closets. Core remains efficient on West. This option prioritises 'feel' and lifestyle over pure area maximization.

**Building Footprint**: 30'-0" × 50'-0"

## Zoning

### Core (West)
- 8'-0" × 13'-0" efficient package
- Lift + stair + landing designed so entry sequence is: North door → immediate open Living volume (core door is side, not frontal)

### Living + Dining
- 24'-0" (almost full width) × 19'-0" deep
- True grand double-volume potential (later 3D)
- North full height glazing + possible side light
- Dining as extension, not separate room

### Kitchen
- 10'-0" × 11'-6" SE
- South light primary
- Open or semi-open to Dining for modern feel (optional partition)

### Master Bedroom (SW)
- 12'-0" × 13'-0"
- South light
- Full Walk-in Closet: 6'-0" × 7'-0" (generous)
- Attached Bath: 5'-6" × 9'-0" (can share wall with walk-in for plumbing efficiency)

### Bedroom 2
- 10'-6" × 11'-6" with 5'-0" × 6'-0" walk-in or large linear wardrobe
- North or South light
- Bath 5'-0" × 8'-6"

### Bedroom 3
- 10'-6" × 11'-0"
- South light preferred
- Bath 5'-0" × 8'-6"
- Linear wardrobe 7'-0"

### Circulation & Feel
- The moment you enter, you experience the full width of the plot as Living space. This is the 'aura and grandeur' the brief demanded.
- Private bedroom zone is clearly separated after the Living volume.
- No door conflicts, no kitchen counter facing the main path.

**Approximate Carpet**: ~1,160 – 1,200 sq ft (slightly less pure room area but higher perceived volume)

**Vastu**: Compliant (SW Master, SE Kitchen, N Living, no NE bedrooms)
**Light**: Strong on primary faces
**Feel / Volume**: Highest of the three options

**Self-Rating**: 8.2 / 10

**Notes for Jury**: This option deliberately trades a few square feet of bedroom for the spatial quality that makes a client say 'wow' on first entry. Walk-ins elevate the lifestyle feel. Recommended when the client values presence and daily experience over maximum room sizes.
