# Delhi Bylaws & Hard Constraints for this Project

## Plot Data (Locked)
- Size: 30 ft (width) × 60 ft (depth) = 1800 sq ft ≈ 167.2 sqm
- Category: 100–250 sqm residential plotted
- Front (North): 20 ft road → 10 ft setback as instructed by Jury (bylaw minimum is 3 m / ~10 ft)
- Rear (South): 20 ft service road → 0 m setback permitted
- Sides: 0 m setback permitted
- Buildable envelope after front setback: 30 ft × 50 ft

## Coverage & FAR (Reference)
- Max Ground Coverage: 75%
- FAR: 300%
- Stilt parking is permitted and not counted in FAR (height limited)
- Typical floors: Upper Ground + First + Second + Third

## Hard Design Constraints from Jury
1. 10 ft North setback must be left empty.
2. Three bedrooms + three attached bathrooms (each bath ≥ 5'-0" × 8'-6").
3. Kitchen + all three bedrooms must receive natural light from North or South face via large sliding windows.
4. Grand entry into Living/Dining — no small claustrophobic lobby.
5. Efficient Stair + Elevator core with usable common landing.
6. Practical Vastu: Master preferably SW, Kitchen preferably SE, no bedroom in NE.
7. Lintel level 9 ft for doors and windows.

## What the Agent Must Never Do Again
- Reduce the plot depth to 50 ft without clearly showing the 10 ft setback zone.
- Place a bathroom or wardrobe between a bedroom and its only light source.
- Create bathrooms smaller than 5 × 8.5.
- Make the entry feel like a narrow lobby.
- Hallucinate dimensions or ignore the light requirement.
