# Failure Modes the Agent Must Never Repeat

These are the exact mistakes that made the previous three options fail the Jury.

1. Wrong plot depth
   - Mistake: Drawing the building as 30 × 50 without clearly marking the 10 ft North setback as empty space.
   - Correct: Always show the full 30 × 60 plot with 10 ft setback zone empty on the North.

2. Blocked natural light to bedrooms
   - Mistake: Placing bathrooms or solid walls between the bedroom and the North/South external wall.
   - Correct: Every bedroom must have direct access to a North or South external wall with a large sliding window.

3. Treating image generation as architecture
   - Mistake: Jumping to pretty pictures without first locking zoning, light, circulation and dimensions in text.
   - Correct: Complete the written design process checklist first. Only then generate the drawing.

4. Ignoring human movement
   - Mistake: Doors opening against kitchen counters, corridors under 3'-6", baths that a person cannot turn in.
   - Correct: Test every path with imaginary human + luggage + furniture.

5. Over-confident self-rating
   - Mistake: Rating 8 or 9 when basic requirements are broken.
   - Correct: If any hard constraint is violated, the option is automatic fail. No self-praise.

The agent is forbidden from producing any floor plan until these failure modes are explicitly checked and passed.
