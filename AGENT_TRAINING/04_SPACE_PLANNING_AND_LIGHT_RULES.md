# Space Planning, Light & Circulation Rules (Final Training)

## Natural Light Non-Negotiable Rule
Every habitable room that requires light (Kitchen + 3 Bedrooms) must have at least one external wall on the North or South face of the building with a large sliding window. 
Bathrooms, wardrobes or internal rooms must never sit between the bedroom and its light wall.

## Preferred Zoning for 30 x 50 Buildable
- North 15-20 ft depth: Grand Living + Dining (entry lands here)
- South face: Prefer Master + Kitchen + one more Bedroom so they all get South light
- West or East strip: Stair + Lift core (keep narrow, 7.5-9 ft)
- Remaining: Third bedroom and circulation

## Core Sizing
- Lift clear car approx 4-5 ft
- Stair (dog-leg or scissor): clear flight width 3'-6" to 4'-0", total core footprint ideally under 110-120 sq ft
- Common landing minimum 5 ft deep where people exit with bags

## Bathroom Rule
Minimum internal clear 5'-0" x 8'-6". Prefer longer dimension along the wall for proper WC + basin + shower layout.

## Entry Feel
The moment the front door opens, the person must experience the full width or large volume of Living. No 6-8 ft wide lobby that feels like a corridor.

## Self-Check List (must be answered Yes before any drawing)
1. Full 30 x 60 plot shown with 10 ft North setback empty?
2. Buildable exactly 30 x 50?
3. All 3 BRs + Kitchen have direct N or S external wall + window?
4. No bath smaller than 5 x 8.5?
5. Entry is grand, not lobby?
6. Master near/in SW?
7. Circulation clear for a person carrying luggage?

Only when all are Yes, generate the plan.
