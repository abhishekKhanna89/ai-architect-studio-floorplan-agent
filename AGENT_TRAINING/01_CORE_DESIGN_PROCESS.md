# AI Architect Agent — Core Design Process (Mandatory)

This is the exact sequence every floor plan must follow. No skipping. No image generation until these steps are completed and documented.

## Step 1: Site & Bylaw Lock
- Plot size locked: 30'-0" width × 60'-0" depth = 1800 sq ft ≈ 167.2 sqm
- Orientation locked: North-facing (front = North)
- Front setback locked: 10'-0" (as instructed by Jury). Buildable depth = 50'-0"
- Rear setback: 0 (as per Delhi MPD for 100-250 sqm)
- Side setbacks: 0
- Ground coverage target: up to 75%
- Stilt parking below, typical floors above

## Step 2: Zoning Hierarchy (Non-negotiable)
1. Public zone first: Grand Living + Dining that receives the entry. Must feel open and grand, not a small lobby.
2. Service core second: Stair + Lift placed so it does not destroy the grand entry or block light.
3. Private zone last: Three bedrooms with clear hierarchy (Master > others).
4. Kitchen placed for work triangle + light + Vastu preference.

## Step 3: Natural Light Rule (Critical — previous failure)
- Kitchen AND all three bedrooms MUST receive direct natural light from either the North face or the South face (or both).
- No bedroom may have its only light source blocked by a bathroom, wardrobe, or internal room.
- Bathrooms may use shafts if needed. Bedrooms may not rely on shafts as primary light.

## Step 4: Dimension Rules
- Attached bathroom minimum: 5'-0" width × 8'-6" length
- Corridor clear width: minimum 3'-6"
- Door clear: 2'-10" to 3'-0" for rooms, 2'-6" for baths
- No random boxes. Every room must have usable furniture zones (bed + wardrobe + circulation).

## Step 5: Circulation Logic
- Entry sequence: Front door → immediate sense of Living volume.
- Private bedrooms accessed via a distinct corridor or zone, not crossing the main Living.
- Kitchen door must not face the main entry path with a counter in between.
- Stair + Lift must have a proper common landing usable by humans with luggage.

## Step 6: Self-Check Before Drafting
Before any drawing is produced, the agent must answer in writing:
1. Is the buildable area exactly 30 × 50 after 10 ft setback?
2. Do all three bedrooms + kitchen get North or South light?
3. Is Master in or near SW?
4. Is there any bathroom smaller than 5 × 8.5?
5. Is the entry grand or lobby-like?

Only if all answers are yes, proceed to drafting.
